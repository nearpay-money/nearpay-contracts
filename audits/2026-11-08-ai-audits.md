# AI audits of 2026-11-08 — findings and resolutions

Two independent AI-agent audits (ADR-0020 decision 6) of `stage-4/4.1-vault` @ `44637ed`
against `lld/contracts-vault.md` v1.3:

- **A1** — fund flows and asynchronous execution
- **A2** — access control, input validation, spec conformance, DoS

Neither found a Critical or High issue. The audit reports are kept by the lead outside this repo
(`audits-vault/audit-1-fund-flows.md`, `audit-2-access-spec-dos.md`). This file records each finding
and what was done about it. The verdicts are the auditors'; the resolutions are the author's and are
open to re-audit.

- contracts `a8902ee`: contract fixes, tests, mocks
- contracts `1880c35`: CI
